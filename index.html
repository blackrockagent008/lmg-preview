Leadership

Led by Experience.
Driven by Results.

Adrian Ramirez, CCIM, serves as Managing Principal of LMG General Contractors, bringing more than 25 years of experience across commercial construction, development, and real estate investment. Under his leadership, LMG has delivered hundreds of millions of dollars in hospitality development across Texas — from flagship Marriott and Hilton properties to independent resort destinations.

A graduate of the Rice University MBA program and holder of the CCIM designation — earned by fewer than ten percent of commercial real estate professionals — Adrian pairs institutional-grade financial discipline with hands-on field expertise. That combination means every LMG project is underwritten, scheduled, and built with an owner's perspective in mind.

His philosophy is simple: build it right the first time. It's why clients return to LMG project after project, and why architects, lenders, and brand representatives across Texas consider LMG a partner of choice.
